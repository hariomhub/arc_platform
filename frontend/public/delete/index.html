Account Management

Delete Your Account

Risk AI Council - riskaicouncil.com

!
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting your account will remove your profile and personal data from Risk AI Council. Posts and shared resources are retained and managed by admin.
1 How to Delete Your Account (In-App)
  • 1
    Open the Risk AI Council app Launch the app on your Android device and log in with your registered email and password.
  • 2
    Go to your Profile Tap your profile icon or avatar in the top-right corner of the home screen.
  • 3
    Tap "Delete Account" On your profile page, tap the "Delete Account" button (shown in red).
  • 4
    Confirm with "Yes" A confirmation popup will appear. Tap "Yes" to permanently delete your account. Your account will be removed immediately.
2 Request Deletion by Email
If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:

support@riskaicouncil.com

Please include your registered email address in the request. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email.
3 What Data Is Deleted or Retained
Data Type Action Retention Period
Full Name and Email Address Deleted Immediately on deletion
Password Deleted Immediately on deletion
Membership Type and Role Deleted Immediately on deletion
Event Registration Details (Phone, Org, Notes) Deleted Immediately on deletion
Posts, Photos, PDFs, YouTube Links and Tags Admin Managed Handled by admin - not deleted automatically
4 Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Account
Yes - you can request partial data deletion. You can request removal of specific data without deleting your full account. Send your request to support@riskaicouncil.com and specify which data you want removed. We will respond within 7 business days.
